# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- First release: twelve decks to read aloud — Would you rather, Never have I ever, Who's most likely to, Get to know you, Hot seat (three questions for one player), Famous dinner, Dilemmas, Absurd debates, This or that, Story starters, Guess who, Time machine.
- 3437 French / 3303 English family cards; opt-in Adults mode adds 409 spicier cards per language (never explicit).
- Deck multi-select, random order without repeats, Previous/Next, optional player names with turn display, "New game" once the deck is exhausted, saved game in progress.
- Interface in English, French, Spanish and German; cards in French or English.
